## **Dangote Cement: Income Statement Model (FY2024 to FY2026E)**

A simple three-year model of Dangote Cement Plc, built in Excel from the company’s own filings. Figures are Group (consolidated), in ₦ million unless stated.

## **What’s in the model**
Group income statement for FY2024 and FY2025, copied from the 2025 Annual Report
Check cells that confirm every subtotal adds up (all show 0)
Growth and margin ratios
A simple FY2026E forecast driven by a few assumptions

## **Key findings**
Revenue grew 20.3%, from ₦3.58 trillion to ₦4.31 trillion
Profit for the year roughly doubled (up 101.7%), from ₦503 billion to ₦1,015 billion
Gross margin rose from 54% to 62%, and net margin from 14% to 24%
Finance costs fell by about half, from ₦700 billion to ₦352 billion
FY2026E: revenue of about ₦5.23 trillion and profit of about ₦1.26 trillion

## **Assumptions**
2026E revenue growth of 21.4%, taken from H1 2026 Group revenue growth and assumed to hold for the full year
Margins and expense ratios held at their FY2025 levels
Other income, impairment, finance income, finance costs and associate income held flat; gain on net monetary position set to zero

## **Sources**
Dangote Cement Plc 2025 Annual Report (consolidated statement of profit or loss)
Dangote Cement Plc H1 2026 Unaudited Results, Nigerian Exchange, 29 July 2026

## **Limits**
This covers the income statement only. It has no balance sheet, cash flow or valuation yet.
